# Carrier Results

A tool to help you understand sickle cell screening results and prepare for a conversation with a healthcare professional.

**Open the app:** [juniorb573.github.io/H2Skill](https://juniorb573.github.io/H2Skill/)

The app is available in English and French. It provides information and support; it does not replace medical advice, make a diagnosis, or predict a child’s health with certainty.

## What you can do

* Select both partners’ carrier results and view the related probabilities.
* Understand the results with explanations and a genetic cross table.
* Explore a family tree and identify information that may be useful to learn.
* View cumulative risk across multiple pregnancies.
* Prepare a summary sheet to bring to an appointment.
* Ask the assistant a question when that feature is available.
* Take a short quiz to check your understanding.

## How to use the app

1. Open the app using the link above.
2. Choose your preferred language.
3. Select the known results for both partners. If a result is unknown, leave that option set to “Unknown.”
4. Review the explanations, probabilities, and other sections.
5. Bring your questions and, if you wish, the summary sheet to a healthcare professional.

Probabilities apply to each pregnancy individually. They are not a diagnosis and should be discussed with a healthcare professional or genetic counselor.

## Chat assistant

The assistant can provide prepared answers. If the artificial intelligence service is available, it can also provide explanations tailored to your question. The app calculates the results and probabilities; the assistant helps explain them.

For severe pain, fever, or difficulty breathing, contact a healthcare professional or emergency services promptly. Do not use the app to make treatment decisions.

## Privacy

The information entered in the app does not include your name. Your browser session is not retained after you close the page. When the artificial intelligence assistant is enabled, the selected results, your question, and a short conversation history are sent to the service to generate a response; according to the project’s configuration, the service does not retain them.

Avoid entering information that could identify you.
